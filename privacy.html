Stashily 貼文收藏 — 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026-10-02

Stashily（以下稱「本 App」）重視你的隱私。本 App 不會蒐集、儲存或分享你的任何個人資料。

資料的儲存方式

  • 你收藏的貼文（文字、作者、發文時間、預覽圖與原始連結）、分類、標籤、備註，以及你加入備註的圖片，都只儲存在你自己裝置的本機儲存空間中。
  • 本 App 沒有自己的後端伺服器，不需要註冊帳號，也沒有串接任何分析工具或第三方廣告服務。開發者無法存取你的內容。

對外連線

為了顯示你收藏的貼文，本 App 會由你的裝置直接連到該貼文所在的平台（例如 Threads、Instagram、X），讀取公開的貼文內容、預覽圖與作者頭像。這些連線不經過我們的伺服器，相關資料處理適用各平台自己的隱私權政策。

分享選單

你在其他 App 透過「分享 → Stashily」收藏貼文時，本 App 只會讀取你分享的那則貼文連結，不會讀取來源 App 的其他內容。

權限

本 App 不會索取通訊錄、定位等與功能無關的權限。只有在你為備註加入圖片時，才會開啟系統的照片選擇器；本 App 只會取得你選擇的那幾張圖片，無法讀取你的其他照片。

資料的刪除

刪除的貼文會先移到「垃圾桶」保留 30 天，之後永久刪除。若你刪除 App，所有本機儲存的資料將一併移除，且無法復原。

兒童隱私

本 App 不會刻意蒐集任何人的個人資料，包括兒童。

政策更新

若本政策有變更，會更新於此頁面並修改上方的日期。

Stashily — Post Bookmarks — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-02

Stashily ("the app") respects your privacy. The app does not collect, store or share any personal data.

How your data is stored

  • The posts you save (text, author, posting time, preview images and original links), folders, tags, notes, and images you add to notes are stored only in local storage on your device.
  • The app has no backend server of its own, requires no account, and uses no analytics or third-party ad services. The developer cannot access your content.

Network connections

To display the posts you save, the app connects directly from your device to the platform hosting each post (e.g. Threads, Instagram, X) to read the public post content, preview images and author avatars. These connections do not go through our servers, and each platform's own privacy policy applies.

Share extension

When you save a post from another app via Share → Stashily, the app reads only the link of the post you shared, and nothing else from that app.

Permissions

The app does not request contacts, location, or other permissions unrelated to its features. The system photo picker opens only when you add images to a note, and the app receives only the images you select; it cannot read your other photos.

Deleting your data

Deleted posts are kept in Trash for 30 days, then permanently deleted. Deleting the app permanently removes all local data, with no way to recover it.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
